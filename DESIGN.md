# Sarıoğlu Orman Ürünleri — Design System

## Overview

**Creative North Star: “Editorial timber catalogue.”**

The website should feel like a small architecture or material studio catalogue rather than a generic SaaS landing page. Product photography carries the page; typography, rules, measured spacing and restrained interaction create hierarchy. The tone is quiet, assured and tactile.

Key characteristics:
- Asymmetric editorial grids instead of repeated card boxes.
- Warm paper surfaces, charcoal type and muted forest green.
- Serif display typography paired with a neutral sans-serif.
- Hairline rules and numbered sections as navigation cues.
- Minimal radii; no pill buttons, glass effects or decorative gradients.

## Colors

- Background: `#f3f0e9`
- Surface: `#f8f6f1`
- Muted surface: `#e8e2d7`
- Text: `#1c211d`
- Muted text: `#66685f`
- Forest green: `#2f4437`
- Deep green: `#1f2e26`
- Wood accent: `#9b7652`
- Rules: `#cfc8bc` / `#9e9a91`

## Typography

- Display: Cormorant Garamond, medium weight. Large, close-set, editorial headings.
- Body/UI: Instrument Sans, regular to semibold.
- Display tracking should stay between `-0.02em` and `-0.03em`.
- Body copy should remain compact and readable, generally below 75 characters per line.

## Layout

- Max content width: 1240px.
- Desktop layouts use asymmetric grid columns and visible alignment lines.
- Section spacing is generous; related elements remain tightly grouped.
- Mobile collapses to a single column without introducing card containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is rare. Use borders or tonal surfaces first. Shadows are reserved for genuine overlays if needed later.

## Shapes

- Small 3px controls.
- 8–12px only where a bounded media container truly requires it.
- Never use pills for primary actions.

## Components

- Buttons are compact rectangular actions with a directional arrow.
- Section headings use a top rule, section index, large serif heading and optional explanatory copy.
- Product cards are unboxed: image, model name and code only.
- Category navigation is a vertical editorial index rather than a grid of promotional cards.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do:
- Let product imagery dominate.
- Use whitespace and rules to establish hierarchy.
- Keep transitions short and functional.
- Keep placeholder imagery until real product assets are supplied.

Don't:
- Nest cards inside cards.
- Use glassmorphism, gradients, glowing effects or oversized rounded containers.
- Zoom product imagery on hover.
- Add decorative icons without a functional purpose.
